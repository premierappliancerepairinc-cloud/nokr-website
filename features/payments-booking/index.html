Nokr takes payments, books jobs online and connects with QuickBooks — all in one place.

An honest note on status: the client portal is live today. Payments, online booking and QuickBooks sync ship with the App Store release. Reserve with Google is further out — it needs a Google partnership. The Roadmap is the status page.

What is included?

  • Payments (in the App Store release) — payments that process inside Nokr (Stripe Connect), so the invoice and the money are in the same place.
  • Online booking (in the App Store release) — clients book a job online, and the booking lands straight in the schedule.
  • Client portal — clients see their jobs, approve quotes and pay invoices, without calling the office.
  • QuickBooks sync (in the App Store release) — connect your QuickBooks Online company and Nokr sends your customers, invoices and payments there, so the books match the work.
  • Built-in accounting (in the App Store release) — Nokr counts the money itself: how much went to parts, how much to sales tax, how much to the technician, how much to the owner. Charts show each technician's average ticket and number of jobs, so the numbers are visible without a separate bookkeeping tool.
  • Reserve with Google (further out — needs a Google partnership) — customers book your service straight from Google Search and Maps. Read more on the Reserve with Google page.

These are the table stakes of a modern CRM — and Nokr states them plainly as such. QuickBooks sync, payments, online booking and portals exist at the competitors too; they are parity, not the reason to choose Nokr. The reason to choose Nokr is Dana: the agent, the voice control, the diagnostics and the client card that grows itself.

One system, no extra tools

A small shop does not need five subscriptions that half-talk to each other — a payment tool, a booking tool, a portal, an accounting sync, and a CRM on top. Nokr puts them in one place, run by the same agent that books the job and writes the invoice. The job, the payment and the books agree, because they were never in separate systems.
