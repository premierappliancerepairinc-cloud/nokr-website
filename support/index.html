Nokr — AI CRM

Nokr Support

Nokr is the CRM that runs itself — built for small field-service businesses. Dana, the AI assistant inside the app, books jobs, keeps customers and job history, creates estimates and invoices, checks van stock, and answers appliance questions from real service manuals.

Need help? Write to us: getnokr@gmail.com We answer from New York, usually within one business day. Please do not send passwords or keys by email.

Getting started

How do I start? Download Nokr from the App Store, or use the web app at app.getnokr.com. Register your company with your work email and you get a 7-day free trial — no card required to try it. After the trial, the subscription is $129 a month for the first seat.

What do I need? An iPhone with iOS 15 or later and an internet connection — or any modern browser for the web app. Nokr works offline too — work you do without signal is queued and synced when you are back online.

Can my technicians use it? Yes. The owner invites team members from the app; each technician gets their own login and sees only their own work and the shared schedule. Each additional seat is $79 a month.

Subscription

How much is Nokr? $129 a month for the first seat and $79 a month for each additional seat, after a 7-day free trial. The price is shown in the app before the first charge — nothing is charged until the trial ends.

How do I pay? In the app: More → Subscription (at the bottom) → Link a card. The card is entered on a secure Stripe Checkout page in your browser — Nokr never sees or stores your full card number. You can replace the card, retry a charge, or reactivate from the same place.

How do I cancel? In the app: More → Subscription → Cancel subscription. Access continues until the end of the current paid period. Cancelling never deletes your data immediately — see "Deleting your account" below.

Where is the subscription in iPhone Settings? Nowhere — Nokr's subscription is managed in the app and on our website through Stripe, not through Apple. That is why there is no subscription entry under your Apple ID settings.

Dana, the AI assistant

What can Dana do? Book and move jobs, look up customers and job history, create estimates and invoices, check parts in stock, write messages, and answer appliance questions from service manuals. Dana marks clearly what is confirmed from a manual and what is only an unconfirmed idea.

Which languages does Dana speak? English, Russian, Spanish and Chinese — and the app itself is available in nine languages: English, Russian, Spanish, Ukrainian, Kazakh, Kyrgyz, Mongolian, Uzbek and Chinese.

Can I talk to Dana instead of typing? Yes — tap the microphone in Dana chat. Your voice is transcribed on your company's server, not by phone dictation, and the audio is deleted right after transcription.

Dana gave a wrong answer. What do I do? Tell Dana — corrections go back into the answer. For sealed-system, gas and electrical work Dana always adds a license-job note; the final call is always the technician's.

Payments and invoices

How do customers pay me? Create an estimate or invoice in the app and send it to the customer. You can record cash or check payments in the app. Card payments from customers need a connected Stripe account — connect it from the payment screen in the app.

Can customers confirm or reschedule online? Yes — open a job and create a client-portal link to send the customer. They can confirm or reschedule from the link.

Does Nokr work with QuickBooks? Yes — connect your QuickBooks Online company from the app and Nokr sends your customers, invoices and payments there so your books match your work. Nokr never reads your books and never changes or deletes anything in QuickBooks. You can disconnect at any time.

Your data

Where is my data? On your company's account on Nokr's server (hosted in Toronto, Canada). Other companies never see it, and Nokr never uses one company's data to benefit another. Full details: Privacy Policy.

Can I export my data? Yes — the owner can export the company's data from the app at any time.

How do I delete my account? In the app: More → Leave company. This cancels the subscription and closes the company. Your data stays available for export for 30 days, then it is permanently deleted. To ask about your data, write to getnokr@gmail.com.

Still stuck?

Write to getnokr@gmail.com — tell us what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. A screenshot helps. Never paste passwords or keys into the message.
