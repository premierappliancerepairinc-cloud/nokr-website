Dana has its own knowledge — built on real service manuals

Most AI answers a question by searching the internet and stitching together what it finds. Dana answers from its own knowledge bases first, built on real service manuals — 380+ diagnostic cards, with the exact numbers a diagnosis turns on. It can search the web when the question needs it. And it keeps learning: the knowledge bases grow as more manuals and diagnostic cases are added.

Every diagnostics answer carries a label — confirmed, from a manual with exact numbers and a source, or unconfirmed, an idea to verify by measurement. So you know what is a fact and what is a guess, on every answer.

How Dana works

Three steps, on every job. No new workflow to learn — Dana fits the way a technician already works.

  1. You speak or photograph

    Say what you need, or photograph a part, a nameplate or a model number. Hands busy, driving, under a sink — it still works.

  2. Dana does it

    Dana books the job, updates the schedule, writes the invoice, checks the van stock, searches for the part or looks up the manual.

  3. You approve

    Nothing is ordered, sent or charged without you. Orders are drafts for your confirmation; invoices go out when you say so.
