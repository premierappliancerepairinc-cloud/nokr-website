What the subscription includes

One subscription covers the whole app. Here is what is in it:

  • Dana — the agent that runs the CRM, finds parts, helps with diagnostics, and learns from real service manuals.
  • Voice control — run the whole app by speaking, hands free.
  • The CRM and the client card — it creates and grows itself from calls, photos and jobs.
  • Van inventory — Dana remembers where every part is, down to the drawer.
  • Parts search and ordering — wholesale and retail prices at once, across your suppliers.
  • Diagnostics and photo part ID — manual-grounded answers, marked confirmed or unconfirmed, and parts identified from a photo.
  • Client portal — your customers confirm or reschedule online.
  • Offline mode — work without signal is queued and synced when you are back online.
  • Payments, online booking and QuickBooks sync — in the App Store release.
  • Geolocation and the team map, mileage and built-in accounting — in the App Store release.
  • AI phone answering (Dana voice dispatcher) and Reserve with Google — further out; see the Roadmap.

How it works — free trial first

The subscription starts with a 7-day free trial with full access. Register in the web app at app.getnokr.com — no card required. Nothing is charged until the trial ends, and the price is shown in the app before the first charge. After the trial, the subscription is $129 a month for the first seat and $79 a month for each additional seat. Cancel any time.
