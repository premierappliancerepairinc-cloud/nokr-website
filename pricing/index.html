Nokr is $129 a month for the first seat and $79 a month for each additional seat, after a 7-day free trial with no card required.

What the subscription includes

Everything in the app, for the whole company: Dana the AI agent (chat, voice, diagnostics from real service manuals), schedule, CRM, estimates and invoices, parts search with retail and wholesale prices, van inventory, team chat, client portal, and offline mode. No tiers, no add-on packs — one subscription, the whole product.

How seats work

The first seat is the owner — $129 a month. Every team member you invite (dispatcher, technician) is an additional seat at $79 a month. A technician who leaves frees their seat when you remove them. The price is always shown in the app before the first charge.
